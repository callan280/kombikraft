KombiKraft does not claim authorship of these resources.

The MIT licence for our original tooling does **not** apply to `assets/` or
the original graphics-derived tables in `native/data/`, or the original BMW
warning text templates in `src/kombikraft/factory/cc_inventory_profile.json`.
The authored catalogue reader and execution tooling remain MIT-licensed.
Inclusion here does
not create an additional patent, trademark, commercial-use or sublicensing
grant. Consult the applicable permission and original terms before reuse
beyond this project. The maintainer is the contact for permission details.

Resource catalogues retain original names, source hashes, offsets and decoding
limits. Stored channels, orientation and proposed vehicle applicability are
not automatically verified. Metadata has been sanitized to remove machine
paths and vehicle identifiers; visual payloads retain their source identity.

The Demon Days sample cover was supplied by the maintainer for the media
preview. It remains third-party album artwork, attributed to Gorillaz / its
original rights holders, and is excluded from the project MIT grant.

Full vehicle firmware, captured operating-system images, BMW runtime libraries,
coding backups, credentials and vehicle-specific diagnostic logs are not
distributed by this visual library. Contributors must supply their own matching
firmware for original-code and native-runtime experiments.
